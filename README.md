### 🧬 About Me & Research Vision
I am an Experimental Sciences high school student passionate about bridging **Evolutionary Biology** and **Computational Genomics**. My goal is to apply data-driven tools and programming to explore biological complexity and publish peer-reviewed research in international youth science journals (*JEI*, *YSJ*).

- 🔬 **Current Focus:** DNA sequence alignment, mutation tracking, and phylogenetic tree reconstruction using `Biopython`.
- 📚 **Academic Approach:** Systematic literature synthesis and computational modeling of evolutionary adaptations.
- 💡 **Beyond Biology:** Developing interactive 3D historical simulations, Android applications, and automated Telegram bots.
