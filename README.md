### 🧬 About Me & Goals
I am a 10th-grade Experimental Sciences high school student interested in connecting **Evolutionary Biology** with **Programming**. I am currently building my foundation in bioinformatics and scientific literature review, aiming to write and publish my first student research paper in international youth journals (*JEI* or *YSJ*).

- 🌱 **Currently Learning:** Basics of `Biopython`, working with biological sequence data, and academic scientific writing.
- 📚 **Interests:** Evolutionary biology, genetics, and ancient Iranian history.
- 💻 **What I Build:** Python scripts, interactive 3D web scenes (Three.js), Android apps (Kotlin), and Telegram bots.
